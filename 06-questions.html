6. Questions for the media team and manager

We have WordPress admin and Yoast SEO on development and production, so coders write the titles, descriptions and alt text and choose keywords. We only ask for real information we cannot make up. Real information already in the design is used as it is.

Audience and goals

  • Answered: our customers are Japanese companies in Japan, and the site is for Japanese visitors.
  • Answered: visitors should be able to contact us, call, apply for a job, browse our works and learn about the company.
  • Which business types and company sizes do we want to reach most?

Services and search topics

  • Which services are the priority? Coders will choose the search words for each service page from this.
  • Should each main service get its own page in the future?

Real company information

  • Official company name, address, phone, email and business hours, exactly as they should appear.
  • Is 「rightcore_manila」 the official Instagram account? Do we have Facebook and X accounts?
  • Are the 12 testimonials real customers who agreed to be shown?
  • Will licensed MV photos and real Works images be supplied, and when?
  • Real questions and answers for the FAQ page.
  • Who provides or approves the privacy policy text?
  • Which Instagram feed plugin may we use, and which Instagram account should it connect to?

Access

  • Who has Google Search Console for the production domain? Can coders get read access?
  • Is analytics (for example Google Analytics 4) installed? Who has access?
  • What is the final production URL? Can we get hosting access to check redirects?

MEO and Google Business Profile

  • Does the company have a Google Business Profile? Who owns and manages it?
  • Who updates photos, posts, hours and replies to reviews?
  • Does the profile show the same name, address and phone as the website?

Approval

  • Do you want to review titles and descriptions before they go live, or can coders set them directly in WordPress admin?
  • Who approves theme file uploads to the production site, and how (chat message, ticket)?

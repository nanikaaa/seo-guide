7. How results will be measured

Technical checks (we control these):

  • Pages indexed and errors: Search Console Pages report [baseline: not available until access]
  • Broken links: link checker result [before ___ / after ___]
  • Page weight and PageSpeed mobile report [before ___ / after ___]
  • Titles, descriptions, canonical, alt: page source checks [pass / fail]
  • Staging hidden, production indexable [evidence]

Business results (influenced, not guaranteed):

  • Search clicks and impressions: Search Console Performance report [baseline date once access is given]
  • Inquiries and calls from the website: analytics or inquiry log [source]
  • Business Profile views and actions
  • Review date: [e.g. 4 and 12 weeks after changes go live]

Note: rankings cannot be promised. Google decides rankings, and changes can take weeks to show.

4. Discussion guide for the coders

A 75-minute meeting. Proposed date: Friday, October 2, 2026.

10 min

Purpose, audience and business goals

Explain

  • The manager wants a report by October 7. The CEO values SEO and MEO. Our job is to find and fix problems with evidence and to set the SEO text and settings ourselves. We do not promise rankings.
  • SEO helps the website appear in Google; MEO is mostly the Google Business Profile, which the website supports with matching company details.

Answers

  • Who are our customers? Japanese companies in Japan. The site is in Japanese.
  • What should a visitor do on the site? All of these: contact us, call, apply for a job, browse our works and learn about the company.

Actions

  • Team lead records our assumptions and sends the open questions in section 6 to the manager and media team.

Expected output and confirmation

  • Questions sent the same day; meeting notes shared with the team.
10 min

Which pages and services people should find

Explain

  • Walk through the site map and page plan (section 2): 11 planned pages, from About to the Contact flow and 404.
  • In development, the homepage, About and News show real content; Works and Instagram show a placeholder (A1). On the test site, every page except the homepage is a 404 (A22).

Questions to answer

  • Go through the SNS decisions in "Decisions needed" (section 2): feed plugin, Instagram account, where the feed appears, redirects.
  • Agree on the proposed order of work.

Actions

  • Team lead records each decision in the page plan; manager and media team confirm priorities and content.

Expected output and confirmation

  • An agreed page list in the report under "Proposed improvements".
20 min

Audit findings

Explain

  • Walk through A1–A22 in priority order: High (A22, A1, A2, A3, A6, A8, A9, A14, A15), then Medium, then Low.
  • Separate "Confirmed issue" (we have proof) from "Needs verification" (we need access or confirmation).

Questions to answer

  • Does anyone disagree with a finding or have more information?
  • Which fixes are safe to do in development and staging before October 7?

Actions

  • Each coder confirms the findings for their sections.

Expected output and confirmation

  • Each finding has an agreed priority; disagreements noted.
10 min

Yoast SEO: our one SEO plugin

Explain

  • The project uses Yoast SEO. It prints the page title, meta description, canonical, share preview (Open Graph), the sitemap (/sitemap_index.xml), breadcrumb and Organization structured data. We do not add a second SEO plugin, and the theme must not print any of these (5.1, C12).
  • Yoast is on the development and production servers, but not in the Local copy (rcrenewalproject) or on the published test site. Pages there do not show what the real site will output.
  • Settings to make are listed in 5.2, section 4: site name and separator, which content types appear in Google, sitemap contents, Organization, Search Console code, breadcrumbs.
  • Yoast's green / orange lights are a writing aid, not a ranking score. Do not repeat keywords to turn them green; write titles and descriptions for people.

Questions to answer

  • Who owns the Yoast settings (one person), and who writes the title and description for each page?
  • Install Yoast in Local and on the test site now, at the same version as the servers?
  • Homepage title wording: company name plus which main service, and 「大阪」 or not?
  • Share image (1200 × 630px) and Organization logo: who makes them from the design?
  • Instagram post type: hide from Google and the sitemap in Yoast until the feed plugin replaces it?

Actions

  • Settings owner applies 5.2, section 4 on development, then production after approval.
  • Each coder fills the Yoast box (SEO title, slug, meta description) for their pages.
  • Theme owner replaces any custom breadcrumb code with yoast_breadcrumb() and removes hard-coded head tags.

Expected output and confirmation

  • Page source of each page shows one title, one description, one canonical and no duplicate JSON-LD.
  • /sitemap_index.xml lists only pages, news and works. Screenshot of each Yoast settings screen in the evidence folder.
10 min

Collaboration and shared files

Explain

  • Shared files: functions.php, header.php, footer.php, assets/css/base/fonts.css. Only one person edits a shared file at a time; others send a request.
  • Collaboration sections (MV, About, Works, Recruit, Instagram) name a lead editor for each change.

Questions to answer

  • How do we avoid overwriting each other's uploads by FTP?
  • Who uploads to staging, and who uploads to production after approval?

Actions

  • Team lead sets one rule: announce in chat before uploading a shared file, and list uploaded files after.

Expected output and confirmation

  • Written upload rule in the team chat.
5 min

Real information from the media team

Explain

  • We have WordPress admin on development and production, with Yoast SEO installed. Coders write titles, descriptions and alt text and choose keywords for each page.
  • We ask the media team or management only for real information: company details, real photos and works, testimonials permission, privacy policy text and the Business Profile. Real information already in the design is used as it is.

Actions

  • Team lead sends the media questions (section 6) and agrees on a delivery date for the real information and images.

Expected output and confirmation

  • Reply from the media team with dates.
10 min

Testing, evidence and deadlines

Explain

  • Every task needs proof: before and after screenshots, page source snippets, PageSpeed or Lighthouse reports, link-checker results.
  • Save evidence in one shared folder named SEO-evidence/[name]/[finding-number].

Deadlines

  • Oct 2 (Fri): meeting; questions sent.
  • Oct 5 (Mon), 18:00: each coder submits checks, quick fixes in development or staging, and evidence.
  • Oct 6 (Tue): team lead reviews and fills the report.
  • Oct 7 (Wed): report submitted. Larger builds (A1 templates) get target dates in the report.

Expected output and confirmation

  • Evidence folder complete; the team lead ticks each task in the report.
